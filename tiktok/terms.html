“There's More To It” is a small tool used by the operator of the There's More To It video channel to upload the channel's own short videos to its own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What the tool does

After the account owner signs in with TikTok and grants permission, the tool sends finished videos to the drafts (inbox) of that TikTok account. Every video is reviewed and published by the account owner in the TikTok app; the tool does not publish anything on its own.

Content

All uploaded videos are original works of the channel. The account owner is responsible for them and for complying with the TikTok Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

No warranty

The tool is provided as is, without any warranty. Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app settings, in the list of apps connected to the account.
