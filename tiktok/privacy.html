Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

“There's More To It” is a private tool used only by the operator of the There's More To It video channel to upload the channel's own videos to its own TikTok account.

What data is used

  • Access token issued by TikTok after the account owner signs in. It is stored only on the owner's own computer and is used solely to upload videos to that account.
  • Basic profile information (display name, open ID), used only to confirm which account the videos are going to.

What is not done

The tool does not collect data about other TikTok users or viewers, does not use analytics or cookies, does not sell or share any data with third parties, and has no server that stores user data.

Retention and removal

The access token is kept until it expires or the owner deletes it. Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app settings, in the list of apps connected to the account.
