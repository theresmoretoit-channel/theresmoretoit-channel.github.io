Terms of Service – There's More To It publishing toolkit

Last updated: 19 October 2026

The There's More To It publishing toolkit is a set of command-line scripts used only by the owner of the YouTube channel There's More To It to manage that channel. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

YouTube Terms of Service

The toolkit uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Its handling of data is described in the privacy policy, which refers to the Google Privacy Policy.

What the toolkit does

After the channel owner signs in with their Google account and grants permission, the toolkit sets the title, description, thumbnail and localizations of the owner's own videos, uploads their caption tracks, adds them to the channel's playlists, and reads the channel's own statistics. It does not access any other channel.

The toolkit is provided as is, without any warranty. Access can be revoked at any time at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
