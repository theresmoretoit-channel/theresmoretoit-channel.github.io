## Let a katastrofa (S01–S02)

**S01 — „On 17 July 2014, a Boeing 777 left Amsterdam for Kuala Lumpur. Flight MH17."** ✅
[DSB] s. 22–23: *„On 17 July 2014, the day of the crash, the subject aeroplane, a Malaysia Airlines Boeing 777-200 with nationality and registration marks 9M-MRD, had arrived at its gate at Amsterdam Airport Schiphol … on a scheduled passenger flight to Kuala Lumpur"*; s. 23: *„The aeroplane took off from Schiphol on runway 36C at 10.31 (12.31 CET)."*

**S02 — „Less than three hours later"** ✅
Vzlet 10:31 UTC ([DSB] s. 23), poslední záznam CVR/FDR 13:20:03 UTC ([DSB] s. 17: *„…ended abruptly at 13.20:03 (15.20:03 CET)"*). Rozdíl **2 h 49 min**. 💬 Drobnost: od odjezdu od gate (10:13 UTC, s. 23) by to bylo 3 h 07 min — „left Amsterdam" se ale přirozeně čte jako vzlet, takže v pořádku.

**S02 — „over eastern Ukraine, it broke apart in the sky."** ✅
[DSB] s. 9: *„The in-flight disintegration of the aeroplane near the Ukrainian/Russian border was the result of the detonation of a warhead."*; *„The aeroplane impacted the ground in the eastern part of Ukraine."*

**S02 — „Two hundred and ninety-eight people died."** ✅
[DSB] s. 9: *„All 298 persons on board lost their lives."* Skladba [DSB] s. 23: *„There were 298 persons, including 283 passengers on board the aeroplane. The crew was composed of four flight crew members and 11 cabin crew members."* Kontrola: 283 + 4 + 11 = 298 ✅.

**S03 — „Twelve years later"** ✅

## Závěr DSB o hlavici (S08–S11)

**S08 — „The Dutch Safety Board answered it in October 2015."** ✅
Stránka DSB: *„Publish date report 13 October 2015"*; titulní strana zprávy *„The Hague, October 2015"*.

**S08 — „A warhead exploded just above and to the left of the cockpit."** ✅
[DSB] s. 9: *„The detonation occurred above the left hand side of the cockpit."* [DSB-PR]: *„a 9N314M warhead … detonated to the left and above the cockpit."* Přesná poloha podle simulace, [DSB] s. 138: *„detonation was 0.25 metres ahead of the aeroplane's nose, 3 metres to the left of, and 3.7 metres above the tip of the nose."* — tedy „just above and to the left" (řádově 4–5 m) sedí.

**S09 — „It sprayed the front of the plane with hundreds of metal fragments."** ✅
[DSB] s. 126: *„The damage observed on the forward fuselage and cockpit area of the aeroplane indicated that there were multiple impacts from over 800 high-energy objects from outside the aeroplane."* [DSB-PR]: *„The forward section of the aircraft was penetrated by hundreds of high-energy objects coming from the warhead."* Materiál: ocel (*„43 of the 72 fragments were found to be made of unalloyed steel"*, s. 95), tedy „metal" ✅.

**S09 — „The three people in the cockpit were killed instantly."** ✅
[DSB] s. 165: *„This impact was only instantly fatal to the occupants of the cockpit."*; s. 166: *„The numerous injuries resulting from perforation of the pre-formed fragments after detonation of the warhead immediately killed the three crew members in the cockpit."*; s. 258: *„…caused the three people in the cockpit to die instantly after the impact of the missile particles."* Tři lidé = kapitán a první důstojník týmu A a purser (s. 258: *„the two pilots and the purser present in the cockpit"*).

**S10 — „Some of those fragments had an unusual shape. A bow tie."** ✅
[DSB] s. 95: *„43 of the 72 fragments were found to be made of unalloyed steel and four of these fragments … had distinctive shapes; cubic and in the form of a bow-tie."* Tab. 11 (s. 92): dva fragmenty tvaru motýlku (č. 10 kokpit, 14×14×4,5 mm, 6,1 g; č. 20 lidské ostatky, 12×12×5 mm, 5,7 g). [SOUD] navíc uvádí *„another seventeen destructive elements … that may have originally been bow-tie shaped"*. „Some" je tedy přiměřené.

**S11 — „That shape comes from one kind of warhead: the one carried by the Buk, a Soviet-designed surface-to-air missile."** ✅ (💬 k nuanci)
[DSB] s. 132: *„the shapes of the pre-formed fragments found in the wreckage and the bodies of crew members in the cockpit; bow-tie and cubes, are only found in the 9N314M warhead"*; tamtéž: *„The Buk surface-to-air missile system is present in this region and is the only weapon system whose missiles have warheads containing … pre-formed fragments in the shape of a bow-tie."* [SOUD]: *„This shape corresponds to the shape of the bow-tie-shaped destructive elements that are used solely in the (new) 9N314M warhead, which is used only in a Buk series 9M38 missile (model 9M38 or 9M38M1)."* a *„a bow-tie destructive element, unique to the 9N314M warhead solely used in a 9M38-series Buk missile"*.
„One kind of warhead" je tedy přesné — DSB i soud říkají „only/solely". 💬 Nuance: DSB srovnával zbraně „present in this region" (tab. 14 a 15), soud to říká bez omezení. A Buk má víc typů hlavic (starší 9N314 motýlky nemá — [DSB] tab. 17, s. 135), takže „the one carried by the Buk" je zjednodušení; význam „ta hlavice, kterou nese Buk" ale neklame. Opravu nenavrhuju.
„Soviet-designed": [DSB] s. 132: *„The Buk became operational in 1979 … The system was designed in the former Soviet Union."* ✅ 💬 Buk je přesně řečeno *systém* (raketa je 9M38/9M38M1); „Buk missile" je ustálené hovorové označení.

## Důkazy a rekonstrukce (S12–S13)

**S12 — „Investigators found those fragments in the wreckage, and in the bodies of the crew."** ⚠️
„Those fragments" navazuje na motýlky z S10–S11. Doklad: motýlek se našel **jeden v troskách kokpitu a jeden v těle jednoho člena posádky v kokpitu** — [DSB] s. 137: *„the bow-tie shaped fragments found in the cockpit and in the body of one of the crew members in the cockpit"*; [SOUD]: *„In the body of one of the crew members in the cockpit, one specific object was found … SIN number AAHJ9117NL, weighing 5.72 grams."* Podle obr. 37 ([DSB] s. 89, „Top right: Captain's body") jde o tělo **kapitána** týmu A. Fragmenty z hlavice obecně byly v tělech **tří lidí v kokpitu** (kapitán, první důstojník, purser; s. 84–85), ne „posádky" — u kapitána týmu B a u člena palubního personálu kovové fragmenty z hlavice nalezeny nebyly (s. 85: *„Cabin crew member: … no metal fragments were found other than a medical implant"*), a [DSB] s. 136: *„Such fragments were not found in the bodies of any other victims."* Posádka má 15 lidí, takže „the bodies of the crew" rozšiřuje nález na lidi, u kterých nebyl.
**Návrh:** `and in the bodies of the cockpit crew.` (pokrývá i 17 fragmentů, které podle soudu původně mohly mít tvar motýlku, z „bodies and pieces of wreckage"). Nejpřesnější varianta, pokud má jít jen o doložené motýlky: `and in the body of the captain.`

**S12 — „And they rebuilt the front of the plane from recovered pieces, to check that the damage fitted."** ✅ / 💬
[DSB] s. 102 (2.19.3 Wreckage reconstruction): *„The reconstruction of the aeroplane's fuselage and parts of the cockpit assisted the investigation … intended to demonstrate the answers to the following questions: From which position relative to the aeroplane did the high-energy objects come? …"*; *„it allowed the investigators to better visualise the recovered wreckage and the damage when comparing the analyses performed with the parts of the wreckage. The assembly of the wreckage into a three-dimensional reconstruction provides … compelling physical evidence"*. Rekonstrukce stála na letecké základně Gilze-Rijen (s. 53: *„partial reconstruction of the aeroplane"*). 💬 DSB výslovně píše, že k dokončení vyšetřování stačily samotné trosky (*„The physical evidence of the recovered wreckage … were sufficient"*), rekonstrukce sloužila k ověření a předvedení — „to check that the damage fitted" je přijatelná zkratka.

**S13 — „So within fifteen months, the weapon was known."** ✅
17. 7. 2014 → 13. 10. 2015 = 14 měsíců a 26 dní → „within fifteen months" sedí. 💬 „Known" = úředně stanoveno závěrečnou zprávou; předběžná zpráva DSB (září 2014) mluvila jen o „high-energy objects" zvenčí.

**S13 — „But a weapon is not a person. It doesn't tell you who fired it."** ✅
[DSB-PR]: *„Additional forensic investigation will be needed to establish the exact launching location; however, such an investigation lies outside the scope of the Dutch Safety Board's mandate."*

**S13b** — výzva k odběru, bez faktického tvrzení.

## Cestující (S14–S18)

**S14 — „For the people on board, it came without warning."** ✅
[DSB] s. 17: *„No aural alerts or warnings of aircraft system malfunctions were heard on the Cockpit Voice Recorder. The communication between the flight crew members gave no indication of any malfunction or emergency prior to the occurrence."*

**S15 — „A blast, a sudden loss of pressure, violent wind and cold, and an aircraft coming apart."** ✅
[DSB] s. 165: *„There was the deafening noise of the impact, abrupt deceleration and acceleration, decompression and the corresponding mist formation, reduced oxygen level, extreme cold, powerful airflow, the aeroplane's rapid descent and objects flying around."* Rozpad: *„The impact of missile fragments and the subsequent pressure wave caused the aircraft to break up."*

**S16 — „The fall lasted a minute to a minute and a half."** ✅
[DSB] s. 162: *„The time interval between the separation of the front fuselage and the moment that the remainder of the aeroplane impacted the ground is estimated to have been 1-1.5 minutes."*; s. 165: *„the one to one and a half minutes for which the crash lasted"*.

**S16 — „Whether anyone stayed aware for part of it, the investigators could neither confirm nor rule out."** ✅ / 💬
[DSB] s. 165: *„It cannot be ruled out that some occupants remained conscious for some time during the one to one and a half minutes for which the crash lasted."* „Neither confirm" DSB doslova nepíše, ale odpovídá to tomu, že nenašel žádný doklad (*„No indications were found that point to any conscious actions. No photographs or (text) messages … were found"*) a že čas smrti určit nešlo (*„It was not possible to ascertain the time at which the occupants died"*). 💬 Míra jistoty: DSB se kloní k rychlé ztrátě vědomí — *„In others, the exposure led to reduced awareness or unconsciousness in a very short space of time"* a u části cestujících *„serious injuries that probably caused their death"*. Formulace „neither confirm nor rule out" samotná může znít jako 50 : 50; vyvažuje to S17 (po opravě níž). Opravu S16 nenavrhuju.

**S17 — „What they did conclude is that there was hardly any time to understand what was happening."** ⚠️
[DSB] s. 165: *„The Dutch Safety Board deems it likely that the occupants were barely able to comprehend the situation in which they found themselves."* (s. 166 a 258 shodně: *„It is likely that the occupants were barely able to comprehend the situation"*). Dva posuny: (1) DSB to nepíše jako závěr, ale jako **pravděpodobnost** („deems it likely"); (2) DSB mluví o **schopnosti** pochopit (opírá se o literaturu o stresu, pracovní paměti a ztuhnutí — pozn. 22–29), ne o **času** — času byla až minuta a půl, což S16 sám říká, takže „hardly any time" vedle „a minute and a half" i vnitřně skřípe.
**Návrh:**
```
What they did judge likely
is that people were barely able
to understand what was happening.
```

**S18 — „Two hundred and ninety-eight people, and their families, are owed the right answer."** ✅ (298 viz S02; zbytek je hodnotící věta).

## Souhrn bloku A

Doloženo bez výhrad: S01, S02 (2 h 49 min; 298 = 283 + 4 + 11), S03 („twelve years"), S08 (13. 10. 2015; detonace 3 m vlevo a 3,7 m nad špičkou nosu), S09 (přes 800 zásahů; tři lidé v kokpitu zabiti okamžitě), S10, S11 („only found in the 9N314M warhead" — DSB i soud), S13 (14 měsíců 26 dní), S14, S15, S16 (1–1,5 min; „cannot be ruled out").

Návrhy oprav:

1. **S12** — `and in the bodies of the crew.` → `and in the bodies of the cockpit crew.` — fragmenty z hlavice byly jen v tělech tří lidí v kokpitu (motýlek doložen v jednom těle, kapitánově); zbytek patnáctičlenné posádky je neměl a DSB výslovně píše, že u ostatních obětí takové fragmenty nalezeny nebyly. (Nejpřísnější varianta: `and in the body of the captain.`)
2. **S17** — `What they did conclude / is that there was hardly any time / to understand what was happening.` → `What they did judge likely / is that people were barely able / to understand what was happening.` — DSB píše „deems it likely that the occupants were barely able to comprehend", tedy pravděpodobnost a schopnost, ne jistý závěr o nedostatku času (a „hardly any time" se tluče s minutou a půl v S16).

Interpretační poznámky bez nutnosti opravy: S11 (Buk má i starší hlavici bez motýlků; DSB srovnával zbraně v regionu, soud bez omezení), S12 rekonstrukce („to check" ~ DSB „demonstrate/compare"), S16 (DSB se kloní k rychlé ztrátě vědomí; vyvažuje S17).

## Místo dopadu (S19–S25)

**S19 — „Then there was the crime scene."** ✅ uvozovací věta.

**S20 — „near Hrabove, in territory held by Russian-backed separatists, in the middle of a war"** ✅
- DSB: „the village of Hrabove … The aeroplane impacted the ground near the village of Hrabove in the eastern part of Ukraine"; trosky na šesti místech „near the villages of Hrabove, Rozsypne and Petropavlivka".
- Kontrola území: DSB 2.11.1 — zapisovače předali „representatives of the armed group present in the area"; odposlech [R] 19:59: „Dubinskiy says Kharchenko should allow the OSCE people access to the crash site" (o přístupu rozhodovala DNR).
- „Russian-backed" + „war": [R] „on 17 July 2014, an international armed conflict between Ukraine and the DPR was taking place on Ukrainian territory, and that the DPR was under the overall control of the Russian Federation." „Russian-backed" je o stupeň mírnější než „overall control" — bezpečné.

**S21 — „International monitors arrived the next day. Armed men decided where they could go."** ✅
- OSCE SMM, zpráva k 18. 7. 2014 18:00: „The SMM obtained very limited access to the crash site of Malaysia Airlines MH17, which came down on 17 July near Hrabove … Some of the ’Donetsk People’s Republic’s‘ guards were visibly intoxicated and aggressive." https://www.osce.org/ukraine-smm/121431
- OSCE 21. 7.: „The SMM was again escorted by armed guards provided by the so-called "Donetsk People's Republic" ("DPR")." https://projects.osce.org/node/121521
- Předseda Stálé rady OBSE Greminger (18. 7.): monitoři nezískali přístupový koridor a zůstali jen asi 75 minut (cit. podle Moscow Times / State Dept.). AP k fotce: ozbrojenci nedovolili prohlédnout místo, kde ležely motory.
- Pozn.: 21. 7. už OBSE hlásí „unhindered access to the site" — věta „decided where they could go" proto sedí na první dny, což scéna tvrdí („the next day"). OK.

**S22 — „The bodies were taken away by train. The flight recorders were handed over four days later."** ✅
- DSB: „the bodies were collected in a refrigerated railway carriage in Torez and then transferred to Kharkiv. The 37 bodies that were originally brought to Donetsk were also transferred to Kharkiv."
- OSCE 21. 7.: „The five train carriages (four of which are refrigerated) … In Torez in the morning of 21 July, the "DPR" had assured the SMM that the train would be allowed to leave for Kharkiv". Vlak odjel z Torezu 21. 7. večer, do Charkova dorazil 22. 7. (scénář datum neuvádí, nevadí).
- DSB 2.11.1: „Individuals unknown to the investigation team removed the two flight recorders from the wreckage area. On 21 July 2014, the recorders were handed over to a Malaysian official in Donetsk, Ukraine by representatives of the armed group present in the area. On 22 July 2014, the recorders were handed over to the Dutch Safety Board in Kyiv". 17. → 21. 7. = **čtyři dny** ✅. DSB dodává: „No evidence or indications of manipulation of the flight recorders were found." (užitečné do komentářů).

**S23 — „Parts of the wreckage were moved or cut up. Dutch investigators could not search the site properly for months."** ✅
- OSCE 21. 7.: „The SMM saw that one of the largest pieces of debris had been split in two. Elsewhere, a considerable amount of smaller debris appeared to have been moved."
- DSB 2.12.1: „It was not until 4 November 2014 that the Dutch Safety Board was able to visit the various locations … Starting on 16 November … wreckage parts were collected during six days" a „many pieces of the wreckage were not physically examined by the Dutch Safety Board until four months after the crash. During this period some parts were removed".
- DSB 1.4: „The first opportunity that was deemed sufficiently safe was from 4 to 22 November 2014, about four months after the crash." Lokalita u Petropavlivky přístupná až 20. 3. 2015.
- Pozor jen na to, že **řezaly i samy nizozemské týmy** při odvozu v listopadu („It was necessary to cut some parts into smaller pieces for transport"). Věta scénáře stojí před „could not search … for months", takže se čte jako zásah před příjezdem vyšetřovatelů — a ten OBSE doložila („split in two"). OK.

2. **„мразь“ ani „воздушное пространство“ v přepisu není.** Je tam **„зараза“** (potvora, mor, svinstvo — o letadle) a **„в наше небо“** (do našeho NEBE). Slovo blízké „vermin“ je **„гад“**, ale to padne až o dvě věty dál.
3. **Řekl „советские офицеры“** — sovětští důstojníci. To je nejsilnější vnitřní doklad záměny a scénář ho teď vynechává.
4. Stíhačku **výslovně nejmenoval**; popsal varování „rádiem, manévry a dávkou stopovek před nosem“, což stíhací zákrok implikuje.
5. **Odvolání ani vysvětlení jsem nenašel** (hledáno rusky i anglicky). Agentstvo uvádí jen, že ho v **květnu 2026** dočasně stáhli z vysílání za jiný skandál a pak se omluvil. Viralita: Agentstvo píše, že video se „в воскресенье“ (neděle 11. 10. 2026) rozšířilo nejdřív na sociálních sítích (odkazuje Reddit r/tjournal_refugees) a pak v médiích (UNIAN). **Anna Komsa / X jsem nedoložil** — scénář ji ale nejmenuje, takže to nevadí.

**S59 — „But it did happen once before.“** ⚠️
U KAL 007 se **nestalo všechno**, co Baranec popsal. Zpráva ICAO 1993 (primární, str. 3 a 39):
- rádio: „**No attempt was made by the USSR to contact the crew of KE 007 by radio** on the distress frequency 121.5 MHz or on any other VHF or HF frequency.“ Osipovič sám: „he did not try to establish radio contact with the aircraft because he would not have had the time to do so, he would have had to tune to that frequency and in so doing he would have lost contact with his ground command.“
- stopovky: „More than 200 rounds were fired, of the armour-piercing variety, **not tracers**, as his aircraft did not have the latter.“ (MO RF tvrdilo, že každý čtvrtý až pátý náboj byl stopovka.)

as Moscow described it at the time.

## Ozvěna „our sky“ ↔ Strelkovův příspěvek

**Originál ji unese, a silněji, než se čekalo.** Baranec neřekl „воздушное пространство“, nýbrž „**залезла в наше небо**“, a hned za tím „**его по-хорошему предупреждали**“. Příspěvek ve „Сводки от Стрелкова“ (v S29 jako *„We warned them. Do not fly in our sky.“*) se běžně cituje jako „Мы же предупреждали — не летать в «нашем небе»“; ověřuje ho jiný blok. **Shodují se tedy oba motivy: varování i „naše nebe“.**

Doporučení: **vrátit**, ale jako součást pokušení, ne jako doklad. Ozvěna je přesně ten důvod, proč klip „vypadal jako chybějící dílek“ (S56), a S61 ji pak rozpustí: v roce 1983 to nebe opravdu sovětské bylo. Tím se z ozvěny stane nejlepší ilustrace pointy S63–S64 („We wanted it to be true“). Návrh (jeden řádek navíc v S56):
```
A few weeks later, the clip went viral.
It even echoed that post: "our sky".
It looked like the missing piece.
So let's check it
the way we checked everything else.
```
Podmínka: S55 musí říkat „our sky“, ne „our airspace“. Riziko: divák si může myslet, že Baranec na příspěvek odkazoval; S61 to musí uzavřít (`There, "our sky" makes sense.`).

**S66 — „So what has actually been proven?"** 💬 Řečnická otázka. V pořádku,
protože S69–S70 hned odlišují „strong indications" od důkazu.

**S67 — „In November 2022, a Dutch court / convicted three men / for their part in the downing."** ✅
[R], shrnutí: *„In the MH17 criminal case, the court acquits one defendant and sentences three accused
to life imprisonment."* Datum 17. 11. 2022. Soud = Rechtbank Den Haag (zasedal v justičním komplexu
Schiphol), „a Dutch court" je přesné.

Za co přesně (výrok, [R] kap. 15, Girkin): *„co-perpetration of, intentionally and unlawfully, causing
an aircraft to crash, although this was likely to endanger another person's life and the act resulted in
a person's death; and … co-perpetration of murder, committed multiple times"* → *„life imprisonment"*.

Nikdo z nich podle soudu nemačkal tlačítko. [R]: *„The Buk TELAR crew members, as those who actually
fired the missile, can each be considered the perpetrator or co-perpetrator of firing the Buk missile."*
Role odsouzených: *„The ranking army commander [Girkin] is responsible, as a functional perpetrator …
Owing to their direct involvement in making it possible for the weapon to be used, the court finds two of
the accused [Dubinskij, Charčenko] to be conventional co-perpetrators."* K Girkinovi navíc: *„the evidence
does not show that Girkin was aware of the availability of a Buk TELAR on 17 July 2014, before it was
fired"*, odsouzen je ale proto, že měl *„the power to decide on the deployment of the Buk TELAR"*.
→ „for their part in the downing" je přesné a nepřehání (jejich podíl = doprava, ostraha, velení,
ne odpal).

**S67 — „Igor Girkin, Sergei Dubinsky / and Leonid Kharchenko."** ✅ 💬
Soud píše *Dubinskiy*, JIT 2023 *„Sergei Dubinskiy"* ([JIT-R], s. 4) i *„Dubinskyi"* ([JIT-PR]).
„Dubinsky" je běžný anglický přepis téhož jména a ve výslovnosti nic nemění. Kdo chce mít
v titulcích pravopis soudu, napíše *Dubinskiy*, ale je to volitelné.

**S67 — „Life sentences, in their absence."** ✅
[R]: *„As none of the accused Girkin, Dubinskiy and Kharchenko, nor counsel representing them, appeared
in court, the cases … were therefore heard in absentia."*

Pravomocnost: [OM-2022]: *„The Netherlands Public Prosecution Service (PPS) has not appealed the
judgement"*, odsouzení se také neodvolali, a proto *„the Public Prosecution Service considers those
judgments also irrevocable"*. [JIT-PR]: *„The verdicts of the court are now irrevocable."*

**S68 — „A fourth man, Oleg Pulatov, / was acquitted."** ✅
[R]: *„The fourth accused is acquitted as he had no direct role of his own in the criminal acts and is
therefore not a co-perpetrator."* Podle soudu *„accept[ed] the use of the weapon"*, ale *„the power of
disposition required for functional perpetration was lacking"*. Osvobození je pravomocné ([OM-2022]:
*„the verdict in Pulatov's case has become irrevocable"*).

**S68 — „That matters, too. / The court weighed each man separately."** ✅
[R]: *„The answer to that question cannot be given in general terms, but requires an assessment of the
concrete acts of the accused considered individually as well as in connection with each other."*
Soud vydal také čtyři samostatné rozsudky (čtyři ECLI). „That matters, too" 💬 je interpretace,
oprávněná.

**S69 — „In 2023, the investigators / reported "strong indications" / that the decision to supply the
Buk / was taken by President Putin."** ✅ (s poznámkou)
[JIT-PR], 8. 2. 2023, první věta: *„There are strong indications that the Russian president decided on
supplying the Buk TELAR to the DPR separatists."* Citované spojení „strong indications" je doslovné.

💬 Zpráva je opatrnější než titulek tiskové zprávy. [JIT-R] 7.2.2: *„strong indications that a decision on
providing the Buk TELAR – or in any event a heavier air defence system with a higher range – to the DPR
was taken at presidential level."* A [JIT-PR]: *„It is not known whether the request explicitly mentions
a Buk system."* Scénář odpovídá doslovnému znění primárního zdroje a S70 hned dodává „are not proof",
takže opravu nenavrhuji. Jen to mějte připravené pro komentáře: JIT netvrdí, že Putin podepsal
konkrétně Buk.

**S70 — „Strong indications are not proof."** ✅
[JIT-PR]: *„Although we speak of strong indications, the high bar of complete and conclusive evidence is
not reached. Furthermore, the President enjoys immunity in his position as Head of State."*

**S70 — „And supplying a weapon / is not ordering it to be fired."** 💬 Logický závěr, vychází
ze zdroje. [JIT-R] 7.2.1: *„It therefore remains unclear what the crew's superior officers in the Russian
military chain of command knew … These superior officers included the commander of the 53rd AAMB, the
Russian Minister of Defence and – as commander-in-chief – the Russian President."*

**S70 — „The investigators could not establish / who ordered the missile to be launched."** ✅
[JIT-R] 7.2.1: *„It is equally impossible to establish whom they received which orders from."*
Soud taky ([R]): *„The case file also fails to identify who gave the instruction to launch a missile, and
why that order was given."* Shrnutí rozsudku v [JIT-R]: *„the identity of the individual who gave the
order to fire, are not known."*

**S71 — „The crew were never identified / well enough to be charged."** ✅
Přesně ta nuance, na kterou upozorňuje uživatel, a scénář ji drží správně. [JIT-PR]: *„the JIT
investigation led to three officers from the 53rd brigade who are named as crew members or their
supervisors. Their involvement in the shooting down of MH17 is not confirmed by other sources.
Therefore, the JIT will not reveal their names."* [JIT-R] 7.2.1: *„second-hand information about the
possible involvement of three former officers of the 53rd AAMB. This information is ambiguous, and thus
far it cannot be confirmed to an adequate extent."* „Identified well enough to be charged" tedy
neříká, že nejsou žádná jména. Říká, že totožnost není prokázaná. ✅

**S71 — „Nor is it known what they thought / they were aiming at."** ⚠️
V úzkém smyslu je to pravda. [JIT-PR]: *„it cannot be ascertained why they fired a Buk missile at MH17,
what their assignment was and what information they had when they fired."* Oba soudy ale řekly víc:
- [R]: *„the court considers it completely implausible that a civil aircraft was deliberately downed …
  the statement of M58 … and the telephone reactions following the downing of MH17 rather show that those
  involved initially thought that they had succeeded in shooting down a Ukrainian military aircraft …
  Therefore, the court will proceed on the assumption that it was believed a military aircraft was being
  downed."*
- [ESLP]: *„the evidence suggested that the missile had been intentionally fired at flight MH17 most likely
  in the mistaken belief that it had been a military aircraft."*

Navíc to vnitřně koliduje se **S33** (*„of what they took for a military plane"*). Divák může z „nor is
it known" vyvodit, že cílem mohlo být i civilní letadlo, a to soud výslovně odmítl jako *„completely
implausible"*.

Návrh (dva řádky jako dosud):
```
Most likely, they took it
for a military plane.
```
nebo, když má zůstat důraz na nejistotu:
```

took it for a military plane.
```

**S73 — „In 2025, two international bodies, / the UN's civil aviation council / and the European Court
of Human Rights, / found Russia responsible."** ✅ (s poznámkou)
- **ICAO:** [ICAO], 12. 5. 2025: Rada *„voted that the Russian Federation failed to uphold its obligations
  under international air law in the 2014 downing of Malaysia Airlines Flight MH17"*. Nároky Austrálie
  a Nizozemska byly *„well-founded in fact and law"*, a to podle čl. 3 bis Chicagské úmluvy
  (*„refrain from resorting to the use of weapons against civil aircraft in flight"*). Nizozemská vláda:
  *„the Russian Federation is responsible for the downing of Flight MH17"*. Písemné rozhodnutí je
  z 30. 6. 2025. ICAO je specializovaná agentura OSN a Rada její řídicí orgán, takže
  „the UN's civil aviation council" je přijatelné zjednodušení.
- **ESLP:** [ESLP], 9. 7. 2025, velký senát, jednomyslně: *„there had been violations of Articles 2, 3
  and 13 in application no. 28525/20 concerning the downing of flight MH17"*. Jurisdikce a přičitatelnost:
  *„Russia was responsible for acts and omissions of the Russian military and of the separatist entities
  in eastern Ukraine"*, a ke konkrétnímu případu *„It was not necessary for the Court to decide exactly
  who had fired the missile since Russia was responsible for the acts of the Russian armed forces and of
  the armed separatists."* Konečné: *„Grand Chamber judgments are final (Article 44 of the Convention)."*
  O spravedlivém zadostiučinění zatím rozhodnuto nebylo.

Odpovědnost jednotlivců a odpovědnost státu scénář nemíchá. S67 mluví o jednotlivcích
(trestní soud), S73 o státu (ICAO a ESLP), a to je správně.

💬 **Co ve scénáři není:** Rusko se proti rozhodnutí Rady ICAO **18. 9. 2025 odvolalo k Mezinárodnímu
soudnímu dvoru** ([ICJ], *Appeal from the ICAO Council decision dated 30 June 2025 (Russian Federation v.
Australia and Netherlands)*, příkaz k lhůtám 27. 11. 2025), a řízení dosud běží. Věta „found Russia
responsible" je i tak pravdivá, protože popisuje, co Rada rozhodla. Opravu nenavrhuji, ale do komentářů
to patří. Rozsudek ESLP je naproti tomu konečný.
